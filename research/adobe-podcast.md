# Adobe Podcast: the facts posts may state

Every fact below was read on Adobe's own pages (podcast.adobe.com,
helpx.adobe.com, adobe.com) on 17 October 2026 (read 2026-10-17). The
podcast.adobe.com pages render in JavaScript inside shadow DOM, so they were
rendered in headless Chromium (Playwright, chromium-1223) and the text,
including shadow roots, was read; the plan comparison table on /features was
also read from a full-page screenshot, because its ticks and crosses are icons.
helpx.adobe.com refuses plain fetches (HTTP 403) and was read the same way.
Pages were read from a UK connection. Anything under **Unverified** must not be
stated in a post. We have not used Adobe Podcast hands-on.

## What it is

- "Adobe Podcast | AI audio recording and editing, all on the web" (home page
  title). https://podcast.adobe.com/en/ (read 2026-10-17)
- Two tools in the Tools menu: **Enhance Speech**, "Remove noise and echo from
  voice recordings", and **Studio**, labelled **BETA**, "Record, edit, and
  enhance in your browser". https://podcast.adobe.com/en/ (read 2026-10-17)
- Home page feature list: Enhance Speech ("Remove background noise and
  echo"), Caption video, Remove music, Transcribe audio and video ("Download
  as text or PDF"), Record a podcast ("Record solo or with remote guests"),
  Edit audio and video ("Cut and compose your story"). "Record, edit, and
  enhance your voice without downloading any software. Use Adobe Podcast right
  in the browser." https://podcast.adobe.com/en/ (read 2026-10-17)
- FAQ: "Adobe Podcast is a web-based tool that lets you record, transcribe,
  edit, and share crisp and clear audio, all in your browser." (dash in
  original) https://helpx.adobe.com/podcast/adobe-podcast-faq.html (last
  updated Apr 15, 2026; read 2026-10-17)
- Available "worldwide, except for China, Cuba, Iran, North Korea, Syria,
  Crimea, and the so-called Donetsk and Luhansk People's Republics." Same FAQ.

## Enhance Speech

- "Enhance Speech makes voice recordings sound as if they were recorded in a
  professional podcasting studio." Page heading "Clean up audio for free with
  Adobe Podcast AI". https://podcast.adobe.com/en/enhance (read 2026-10-17)
- FAQ: Enhance Speech "is language agnostic and does not generate or
  translate across different languages." "This tool enhances audio by
  filtering out noise and artifacts, adjusting pitch and volume levels, and
  normalizing the audio." Clearer input gives better output.
  https://helpx.adobe.com/podcast/adobe-podcast-faq.html (read 2026-10-17)
- FAQ: in Studio, "Currently, you can only enhance at the project level" (not
  per speaker). Same FAQ.
- Enhance Speech v2 (Feb 2025 update): "clearer, more natural-sounding
  dialogue"; a **speech slider** (how much enhancement to apply to the
  isolated speech) and a **background slider** (loudness of the isolated
  background noise). https://podcast.adobe.com/en/guides/latest-updates
  (last updated Feb 26, 2025; read 2026-10-17)
- Enhance Speech v2 is also available inside Studio, applied to tracks.
  https://podcast.adobe.com/en/guides/whats-new-in-adobe-podcast-may-2025
  (last updated June 26, 2025; read 2026-10-17)
- March 2026, **Premium-only**: "advanced source separation", independent
  control of speech, background noise and music; a music control slider "to
  adjust, rebalance, or mute background music"; "Download stems (isolated
  audio tracks) for speech, background noise, and music."
  https://podcast.adobe.com/en/guides/whats-new-in-adobe-podcast-march-2026
  (last updated March 5, 2026; read 2026-10-17)
- Remove music page: "Remove background music from any audio file or video
  file"; "Download separate tracks for speech, music, and ambience"; "Included
  with Adobe Firefly Pro." https://podcast.adobe.com/en/remove-music-from-audio-or-video-with-adobe-podcast
  (read 2026-10-17)
- Enhance Speech guide: reduces background noise, "Reduce hums, clicks,
  static, and more". https://podcast.adobe.com/en/guides/how-enhance-speech-can-improve-your-recording-sound-quality
  (read 2026-10-17)
- Enhance Speech has two versions, v1 and v2; Adobe suggests trying both on a
  short clip. Works "on desktop or mobile".
  https://podcast.adobe.com/en/guides/what-is-enhance-speech (last updated
  April 5, 2025; read 2026-10-17)
- Download format: "When downloading from Enhance Speech, we do not convert
  the format. Whatever format you import, you'll receive back in the
  download." https://helpx.adobe.com/podcast/technical-requirements.html
  (last updated Aug 4, 2025; read 2026-10-17)
- Enhance Speech import formats: audio wav, mp3, m4a, aac, flac; video mp4,
  mov, m4v; up to 1 GB. Same technical requirements page.

## Studio and transcript-based editing

- "Adobe Podcast Studio transcribes every word using the same
  industry-leading transcription as Adobe Premiere Pro. Simply cut, copy, and
  paste your audio just like a text document."
  https://podcast.adobe.com/en/ (read 2026-10-17)
- Edit page: "Each word is highlighted as it's spoken"; "You can cut, copy,
  and paste just like a text document to edit your project." Transcript
  mistakes can be corrected. https://podcast.adobe.com/en/edit-with-adobe-podcast
  (read 2026-10-17)
- FAQ: multitrack audio and video can be uploaded to Studio, "each track will
  remain separate for editing"; "simply delete text in the transcript to
  remove the corresponding audio or video from your project."
  https://helpx.adobe.com/podcast/adobe-podcast-faq.html (read 2026-10-17)
- Video in Studio: "To remove a word or phrase, simply delete it from the
  transcript, your audio and video will update automatically. You can also
  rearrange or trim sections, just cut, copy, and paste" (dashes in original
  replaced with commas). Video uploads up to 5 GB and 2 hours.
  https://podcast.adobe.com/en/guides/edit-videos-in-adobe-podcast-studio
  (last updated June 6, 2025; read 2026-10-17)
- Speaker detection (diarisation): select "Detect speakers" when
  transcribing; otherwise one speaker. "Some users may not see the Detect
  speakers option depending on their plan or region." Not on EDU plans.
  https://helpx.adobe.com/podcast/adobe-podcast-faq.html (read 2026-10-17)
- Remote recording: share a link; each participant's audio "is captured
  directly in their browser and continuously saved"; optional video;
  "separate, high-quality tracks for each speaker" after the session. Studio
  captures "individual tracks in 16-bit 48k WAV". FAQ and
  https://podcast.adobe.com/en/ (read 2026-10-17)
- March 2026: video recording rolled out in remote sessions; multitrack
  upload for audio and video with automatic sync "with a single unified
  transcript"; imports from Zoom, Restream, StreamYard or Riverside.
  https://podcast.adobe.com/en/guides/whats-new-in-adobe-podcast-march-2026
  (read 2026-10-17)
- Royalty-free music library for intros, outros and background; upload your
  own music ("Your Music"). https://podcast.adobe.com/en/ and
  https://podcast.adobe.com/en/guides/latest-updates (read 2026-10-17)
- Captions on video, styled with themes, "Supports captions in English,
  Hindi, German, Italian, Portuguese, French, and Spanish"; speaker names can
  be shown or hidden. https://podcast.adobe.com/en/add-captions-to-video-with-adobe-podcast
  (read 2026-10-17)
- Audiograms (audio to captioned video with themes, custom backgrounds on
  Premium). https://podcast.adobe.com/en/convert-audio-to-video-with-adobe-podcast
  (read 2026-10-17)
- SRT subtitle export for video, export clips, Hindi transcription (August
  2025 update). https://podcast.adobe.com/en/guides/whats-new-in-adobe-podcast-august-2025
  (last updated August 20, 2025; read 2026-10-17)
- Project templates, shared by link. FAQ.

## Filler words, pauses and transcript clean-up (the key question)

What Adobe's Adobe Podcast pages say, in full, about filler words and pauses:

- "Using the text-based editing tool in Studio can help you easily identify
  filler words in your recording, allowing you to listen to and take action
  where these words appear. Removing every filler word might sound unnatural,
  though, so make sure you pick and choose which ones to cut based on how the
  recording sounds as a whole."
  https://podcast.adobe.com/en/guides/from-rough-to-ready-a-beginners-guide-to-podcast-editing
  (last updated June 20, 2025; read 2026-10-17)
- "Transcriptions provide a bird's eye view of an audio recording ... Quickly
  catch redundancies, filler words, or awkward pauses and make the choices you
  want to for editing them."
  https://podcast.adobe.com/en/guides/a-beginners-guide-to-text-based-editing
  (last updated June 20, 2025; read 2026-10-17)
- Feb 2025: Studio transcription "delivers more accurate results, including
  filler words". https://podcast.adobe.com/en/guides/latest-updates (read
  2026-10-17)
- "You can cut awkward pauses or tighten up an answer just by editing the
  transcript". https://podcast.adobe.com/en/guides/why-your-podcast-needs-transcripts
  (read 2026-10-17)
- An editing guide advises you to "eliminate elements like filler words,
  stutters, and any other pauses" yourself, and to remove redundant material
  by listening. https://podcast.adobe.com/en/guides/from-messy-audio-to-smooth-storytelling
  (read 2026-10-17)

What none of the Adobe Podcast pages read describes: a button or setting that
detects and removes filler words automatically, a "remove all" for fillers, a
pause or silence shortener, or any AI that reads the transcript and proposes
cuts for false starts, repetition or meaning. The plan comparison table on
https://podcast.adobe.com/features lists "Transcription" and
"Transcription-based editing" (both plans) and has no filler-word or pause row.
Pages checked for this: home, /features, /en/enhance, /en/edit-with-adobe-podcast,
/en/transcribe-audio-with-adobe-podcast, /en/record-your-podcast-for-free,
captions, audiogram and remove-music pages, the FAQ, technical requirements,
the plan change FAQ, and these guides: latest-updates (Feb 2025), May 2025,
August 2025 and March 2026 what's new, from-rough-to-ready, a beginner's guide
to text-based editing, from-messy-audio-to-smooth-storytelling,
edit-videos-in-adobe-podcast-studio, why-your-podcast-needs-transcripts,
what-is-enhance-speech, how-enhance-speech-can-improve-your-recording-sound-quality,
how-to-record-a-podcast-with-adobe-podcast, adobe-express.

So the defensible statement is: **Adobe Podcast's own pages document
transcripts that include filler words, which you remove yourself by deleting
them from the transcript; they do not document automatic filler-word removal,
pause shortening, or an AI clean-up of the transcript.** Whether Studio
highlights filler words in the transcript on its own ("help you easily
identify") is not spelled out; see Unverified.

### Premiere Pro (a separate Adobe product)

- "Text-Based Editing in Adobe Premiere lets you detect pauses in speech and
  bulk delete fillers, text, or pauses in transcripts." Filter icon in the
  Transcript panel; choose Text, Filler words, Pauses or Speakers; "Delete" for
  one instance or "Delete all" for bulk.
  https://helpx.adobe.com/premiere/desktop/edit-projects/edit-video-using-text-based-editing/detect-and-delete-pauses-in-transcripts.html
  (last updated Jan 7, 2026; read 2026-10-17)
- Premiere is a desktop application (help URL path "premiere/desktop"). Its
  price and plan were not read: do not state them.
- The only link Adobe Podcast's pages draw to Premiere is that Studio uses
  "the same industry-leading transcription as Adobe Premiere Pro".

## Plans and prices

- **Standalone Adobe Podcast Premium is no longer sold to new customers.**
  "Starting August 30, 2026, Adobe will no longer be selling Adobe Podcast
  Premium as a standalone plan to new members." Existing subscribers keep
  Premium at their current price and get Firefly Standard added "at no extra
  cost" with 2,000 generative credits a month. Checkout on podcast.adobe.com
  on or after that date prompts "the Firefly Pro plan, which includes
  everything Podcast offered (Enhance Speech and more)." If an existing
  subscriber cancels after 30 August 2026 they need "the Firefly Pro plan to
  regain access to Podcast Premium features." Reason given: "By bringing Adobe
  Podcast into Firefly, we can build on everything you already use and expand
  into new AI audio capabilities like generating music, sound effects, and
  speech." Enhance Speech "is not going anywhere".
  https://helpx.adobe.com/podcast/adobe-podcast-premium-plan-change-faq.html
  (last updated Jul 30, 2026; read 2026-10-17)
- Firefly Pro, per that FAQ: everything in Podcast, plus Firefly generative
  tools "such as AI audio (soundtrack, speech, sound effects), image creation,
  and video tools, with 4,000 generative credits/month". Same page.
- podcast.adobe.com plans page: "Unlock more with the Adobe Firefly Pro plan
  with access to Adobe Podcast Premium". Firefly Pro shown at **$19.99 /
  month** with "Start free trial". The page does not say whether tax is
  included and shows no annual price. https://podcast.adobe.com/features
  (read 2026-10-17; /en/plans shows the same header)
- Adobe UK Firefly plans page: Firefly Pro **£19.99/mo incl. VAT, Monthly**,
  4,000 generative credits a month, "Free trial". Other tiers on the page:
  Firefly Standard £9.98/mo (2,000 credits), Firefly Pro Plus £47.99/mo
  (10,000), Firefly Premium £200.40/mo (50,000), all incl. VAT, monthly; Pro
  Plus and Premium had a 30% first-year offer ending 21 Oct. The Firefly plans
  page itself does not mention Adobe Podcast. Adobe Express Premium is ticked
  for Pro, Pro Plus and Premium (not Standard). "Generate Speech" is listed
  among premium Firefly features that use generative credits.
  https://www.adobe.com/uk/products/firefly/plans.html (read 2026-10-17)
- Credits: "Adobe Firefly plans include a monthly allocation of generative
  credits that reset each billing cycle and do not roll over." Same page.
- "The Adobe Firefly Pro plan also includes premium features on Adobe
  Express." https://podcast.adobe.com/features (read 2026-10-17)

### Free plan vs Firefly Pro (https://podcast.adobe.com/features, read 2026-10-17)

upload one at a time", "No strength adjustment", "30 minutes max duration (up
to 500 MB), 1 hour max per day"; Studio: "Download projects up to 30 minutes, 2

## Formats, limits, platforms, languages

- Studio import: audio .mp3 .wav .m4a .flac .ogg .aif .3gpp; video .mp4 .mov
  .m4v .3gpp .webm; up to 5 GB and 2 hours per file (video up to 4K).
  https://helpx.adobe.com/podcast/technical-requirements.html (read 2026-10-17)
- Studio export: audio .mp3 or .wav (WAV 48 kHz, 16-bit; MP3 48 kHz); video
  .mp4 1080p or 720p, H.264; transcript .txt, .pdf, .docx; audiogram .mp4.
  Premium users can download separate tracks per speaker. Same page and FAQ.
  SRT subtitles for video (August 2025 guide).
- Browsers: "Studio is only supported on Google Chrome (desktop)." Enhance
  Speech supports the latest Chrome, Safari, Edge and Firefox "on desktop and
  mobile". Same technical requirements page.
- Transcription languages: English, French, German, Hindi, Italian,
  Portuguese, Spanish (seven). Interface languages: Bengali, English,
  Filipino, French, German, Hindi, Indonesian, Italian, Portuguese, Spanish,
  Turkish. Same page.

## Speech generation

- Adobe Podcast's own pages (home, Enhance, Studio use-case pages, FAQ,
  technical requirements, guides above) describe no AI voice, voice cloning,
  overdub or text to speech in Studio or Enhance Speech. The FAQ says Enhance
  Speech "does not generate or translate".
- The Firefly Pro plan, which is now how new customers get Podcast Premium,
  includes Firefly's "Generate Speech" (with generative credits) and Adobe
  says it is bringing Podcast into Firefly to "expand into new AI audio
  capabilities like generating ... speech". So: generated speech is part of
  the plan, not described as part of Adobe Podcast itself.

## Adobe Express and Premiere Pro

- Express: Firefly Pro "also includes premium features on Adobe Express"
  (podcast.adobe.com/features); audiogram page suggests "Try Adobe Express to
  design your own backgrounds". Firefly plans page ticks Adobe Express Premium
  on Firefly Pro.
- Premiere Pro: Studio uses "the same industry-leading transcription as Adobe
  Premiere Pro" (home page). Premiere's own text-based editing documents
  filler and pause detection with "Delete all" (see above).
