# Audacity: the facts posts may state

Every fact below was read on Audacity's own pages (audacityteam.org,
support.audacityteam.org, plugins.audacityteam.org) or the company's audio.com
pages on 17 October 2026, unless marked otherwise, and re-checked by a
second pass the same day (GitHub release data for the OpenVINO plugin is
from the repos the plugin download page links to). Anything under
"Unverified" must not be used in a post.

Note on versions: the current release is Audacity 4.0.1. Much of
support.audacityteam.org is still the Audacity 3 manual (paths under `/au3/`).
Where a fact comes from the Audacity 3 manual, it is marked "(AU3 manual)".

## Editing model

- Waveform editing: import a file or record, and "you will see a waveform of
  your sound". To remove a section, select it "by clicking and dragging in the
  waveform", then press Delete or Backspace.
  https://support.audacityteam.org/basics/audacity-editing (read 2026-10-17)
- Clips can be moved by dragging the clip header, split with the split tool
  (S), trimmed with handles. "Trimming is a non-destructive operation."
  Clips can be sped up or slowed down, and pitch changed with an "optimize for
  voice" option. Same URL (read 2026-10-17)
- Cut page: select by click and drag, Ctrl+X / Cmd+X to cut, "Audacity
  supports unlimited undo throughout the editing session." Lists "Remove ums,
  coughs, and long pauses from spoken recordings" as a podcast use of Cut.
  https://www.audacityteam.org/features/cut/ (read 2026-10-17)
- Audacity 4 replaces Sync-lock with ripple editing ("Delete and close gap
  (all tracks)"). Multiple clips can be selected with Shift+Click.
  https://support.audacityteam.org/new-in-audacity-4/audacity-3-to-4-transition-guide
  (read 2026-10-17)
- Multitrack: Audacity 3 manual describes it as a "multi-track audio editor
  and recorder" (AU3 manual).
  https://support.audacityteam.org/au3/basics/downloading-and-installing-audacity
  (re-read 2026-10-17). The Audacity 4 manual's mixing page: "You can adjust
  volume and panning per track".
  https://support.audacityteam.org/audio-editing/mixing-and-panning-tracks
  (re-read 2026-10-17). The Audacity 4 page also shows per-track metering.
- Projects saved as .aup3 keep realtime effects editable and clips
  untrimmable. https://support.audacityteam.org/basics/saving-and-exporting-projects
  (read 2026-10-17)

## Noise reduction and effects

- Noise Reduction: "Remove background noise from audio for free. Eliminate
  hiss, hum, fan noise, and buzzing." https://www.audacityteam.org/features/
  (read 2026-10-17)
- Steps: select noise only, Effect > Noise Reduction > Get Noise Profile,
  select the audio to clean, apply. Works best on steady noise (room/preamp
  hiss, fan, 50/60 Hz hum, buzzing, tape hiss). For "non-stationary noise such
  as traffic, crowd chatter, or background music" other tools usually work
  better. Heavy settings can sound watery or muffled. "Noise reduction
  improves recordings but cannot fully substitute for good recording
  conditions." https://www.audacityteam.org/features/noise-reduction/
  (read 2026-10-17)
- Noise Gate is also on the current feature page ("Silences audio that falls
  below a set volume threshold ... Found under Effect > Gate"), with spectral
  editing, Filter Curve EQ and MuseHub plugins as alternatives. The same page
  says "Works offline".
  https://www.audacityteam.org/features/noise-reduction/ (re-read 2026-10-17)
- Notch Filter, with the tip that mains hum "is 60Hz in north and middle
  America, and 50Hz in most other countries" (AU3 manual only).
  https://support.audacityteam.org/au3/repairing-audio/noise-reduction-removal
  (re-read 2026-10-17)
- Compressor (destructive or realtime), with factory presets.
  https://support.audacityteam.org/audio-editing/reducing-dynamic-range-compressor-limiter
  (read 2026-10-17)
- Loudness Normalization sets "the target loudness required by podcast
  platforms, television/radio programmes and some websites", in LUFS
  (AU3 manual only; there is no Audacity 4 manual page for it and the
  Audacity 4 page does not mention it).
  https://support.audacityteam.org/au3/audio-editing/loudness-normalization
  (re-read 2026-10-17)
- Audacity 4 page, "Effects, redesigned": Compressor, Filter Curve, Graphic
  EQ, Limiter, Reverb. https://www.audacityteam.org/audacity-4/ (re-read
  2026-10-17)
- Feature list includes reverb, distortion, compressor, delay, echo, pitch
  changer, speed changer, reverse, vocal remover.
  https://www.audacityteam.org/features/ (re-read 2026-10-17)
- Realtime effects and destructive effects both exist. The Audacity 4 manual
  page lists supported plugin formats as "Audio Units (macOS only), VST3,
  VST, LV2, and LADSPA".
  https://support.audacityteam.org/audio-editing/using-realtime-effects
  (re-read 2026-10-17)
- Plugins in Audacity 4: "Open-standard LV2 plugins load right alongside VST3
  and Audio Units". MuseHub effects "sit in the same effect chain".
  https://www.audacityteam.org/audacity-4 (read 2026-10-17)
- Homepage: "a vast selection of third-party plugins, including VST3, Nyquist
  and more." https://www.audacityteam.org/ (read 2026-10-17). Not
  version-specific: Nyquist is not in the Audacity 4 manual's plugin list
  above, and the Audacity 3 to 4 transition guide (written at Alpha 1) says
  Nyquist "may be (re-)added in the future". Do not state Nyquist support for
  Audacity 4.

## AI features and transcription (plugins, not built in)

- Transcription feature page: "Transcribe speech to text locally with Whisper
  AI. No uploads, no minute limits, no subscription." Step 1 is "Install the
  free OpenVINO AI plugin for Audacity". Whisper "writes every word into a
  label track you can edit, search, and export as SRT subtitles, VTT, or plain
  text." "Whisper supports 99 languages." Runs Whisper "entirely on your own
  computer". Also a Translate mode (into English) and an experimental
  speaker diarization model (small.en-tdrz). Export labels via "File →
  Export Other → Export Labels (SRT, VTT, or TXT)". The page does not
  describe editing the audio by editing the transcript.
  https://www.audacityteam.org/features/transcription/ (re-read 2026-10-17)
- OpenVINO plugin download page: separate installers for Windows and macOS.
  Effects: music separation, noise suppression ("Works best on spoken word
  audio"), music generation and continuation, Whisper transcription, audio
  super resolution. **Warning on the page: "The OpenVINO plugins are not yet
  compatible with the latest Audacity version".** Download links are builds for
  Audacity 3.7.1 (`audacity-win-v3.7.1-R4.2-64bit-OpenVINO-AI-Plugins.exe`,
  `Audacity-OpenVINO-3.7.1-R4.2.pkg`).
  https://www.audacityteam.org/download/openvino/ (read 2026-10-17)
- The download page's links go to two GitHub repos. Windows build: Intel's
  repo, release v3.7.1-R4.2, published 2024-12-20 (latest there).
  https://api.github.com/repos/intel/openvino-plugins-ai-audacity/releases
  (re-read 2026-10-17). macOS build: audacity/mod-openvino-macos release
  v3.7.1-R4.2-beta-3, a pre-release published 2025-06-19.
  https://api.github.com/repos/audacity/mod-openvino-macos/releases
  (read 2026-10-17). So "latest release is December 2024" is only true of
  the Windows build.
- The Audacity 4 Alpha 1 release notes listed "OpenVino plugin support" among
  features "in development and will be included in the main release of
  Audacity 4.0". The download page warning (above) is the current statement.
  https://support.audacityteam.org/new-in-audacity-4/audacity-4-alpha-1-release-notes
  (read 2026-10-17)
- AI plugins page: "These AI features run 100% locally on your PC ... no
  internet connection necessary!" Run on CPU, GPU or NPU.
  https://plugins.audacityteam.org/ai-plugins/ai-plugins (read 2026-10-17)
- Same page lists NVIDIA Broadcast as an AI denoiser option: Windows only, NVIDIA
  RTX GPU required, spoken word only.
  https://plugins.audacityteam.org/ai-plugins/ai-plugins (read 2026-10-17)
- Conflict to be honest about: the transcription feature page says "Free for
  Windows, Mac & Linux" next to a 4.0.1 download button, while the plugin
  download page warns the plugins are not yet compatible with the latest
  version and offers only Windows and macOS 3.7.1 builds. Posts state the
  download page warning and that the builds are for 3.7.1.
- MuseHub plugins linked from Audacity's site footer and homepage "Top-5
  trending plugins": "Soap Voice Clean & Capture Bundle" ("Clean up spoken
  voice and create instant transcripts"), MuseFX ("Essential collection of
  mix effects"), Speaker Splitter Pro ("Automatically split mixed-speaker
  audio into separate tracks"), Trinity EQ, Graillon 3 ("Real-time vocal
  tuner"). https://www.audacityteam.org/ (re-read 2026-10-17). Audacity's
  pages do NOT say these are paid; MuseFX/"Muse FX" is described as a free
  extra (above). Whether each is paid, and prices, are unverified.

## Exporting

- File > Export Audio. Tips mention MP3, Opus, M4A (AAC), WAV, FLAC and
  Wavpack; "You may need to install FFMPEG to access some of these options."
  https://support.audacityteam.org/basics/saving-and-exporting-projects
  (read 2026-10-17)
- "Due to patent restrictions, FFmpeg cannot be distributed with Audacity
  itself. However, FFmpeg is required to import and export a variety of audio
  formats, including M4A and WMA." LAME for MP3 is now included on Windows and
  macOS. https://support.audacityteam.org/basics/installing-ffmpeg
  (read 2026-10-17)
- Import: drag and drop "for example, an MP3 or WAV"; M4A or WMA import needs
  FFmpeg. https://support.audacityteam.org/basics/audacity-editing
  (read 2026-10-17)
- Labels can be exported (File > Export Other > Export Labels).
  https://www.audacityteam.org/download/openvino/ (read 2026-10-17)

## Cloud (audio.com)

- "Audio.com, the online companion to Audacity", for collaborating and
  versioned backups. https://www.audacityteam.org/ (read 2026-10-17)
- File > Save to Cloud, link an audio.com account. "Cloud projects have
  backups and versioning ... you can easily share them with collaborators."
  https://support.audacityteam.org/basics/saving-and-exporting-projects
  (read 2026-10-17)
