# Podcastle (now Async): the facts posts may state

Every fact below was read on the vendor's own pages on 17 October 2026
("read 2026-10-17"). Pages were rendered in a headless browser so that
JavaScript-loaded prices and the Monthly/Yearly toggle could be read. Anything
not listed here is not to be claimed. Quotes are verbatim except that dashes
in the original have been replaced with commas or colons.

## Plans and prices

Source for this whole section: https://async.com/pricing, read 2026-10-17.

- Prices displayed to us **in GBP**. The page may localise currency; we only
  saw GBP. Free plan's comparison column showed "$0.00", every paid price
  showed £.
- Plans: Free, Essentials, Pro ("Most popular"), Teams ("Collaboration"),
  Business ("Custom", "Contact us").
- Banner: "SAVE 40% WITH YEARLY PLANS". The Yearly toggle is the default.

| Plan | Monthly toggle | Yearly toggle (per month shown) | "Save" shown on yearly |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Free | £0.00 | £0.00 | n/a |
| Essentials | £19.99 | £11.99 | Save £96 |
| Pro | £39.99 | £23.99 | Save £192 |
| Teams | £84.99 per creator | £49.99 per creator | Save £420 |
| Business | Custom | Custom | n/a |

- The page shows per-month figures for both toggles. It does not print an
  annual total. Our arithmetic (label it as ours): £11.99 x 12 = £143.88,
  £23.99 x 12 = £287.88, £49.99 x 12 = £599.88. The "Save" figures equal
  (monthly minus yearly) x 12, e.g. (£19.99 - £11.99) x 12 = £96.
- "Teams" price is labelled "per creator".
- Paid plan buttons read "Start free trial" on the Yearly toggle and "Get
  started" on the Monthly toggle. Footnote: "* Actual credits will be
  activated after trial payment is done, until that you will have 50
  credits." Trial length: **not stated on the page, unverified.**

### Plan cards (bullets as listed)

- Essentials, "For solo creators getting started": 450 credits / month;
  "4 hours of Audio & Video Recording"; "2 hours of Text-to-Speech"; "20 GB
  of Cloud Storage"; "AI Audio enhancement: Magic Dust, Noise reduction,
  Silence removal"; "AI Subtitles (English only)"; chat-based video editing;
  video, images, voice, music and sound effects generation.
- Pro, "For professional creators who ship weekly": 1200 credits / month;
  "20 hours of Audio & Video Recording"; "10 hours of Text-to-Speech";
  "120 GB of Cloud Storage"; same audio enhancement; "AI Subtitles (English
  only)"; "AI Video Enhancement: AI video enhance, Eye contact, Background
  remover".
- Teams, "For small teams producing at volume": 3000 credits / month;
  "50 hours of Audio & Video Recording"; "40 hours of Text-to-Speech"; "1 TB
  of Cloud Storage"; "AI Subtitles (5+ languages)"; "AI Dubbing & Lipsync";
  "Producer Mode"; "Brand Kit"; "Basic user management".
- Business: custom hours, custom credits, "Advanced user management",
  "Priority support", "Advanced security & compliance".
- Discrepancy, flag it if used: the Essentials card says "4 hours of Audio &
  Video Recording" but the comparison table row "Remote multitrack recording
  (hours)" says 2 hours for Essentials. Pro (20) and Teams (50) agree.

  your multi-track audio files directly from a transcript."

  edit by deleting words from an automatic transcript instead of cutting
  waveforms manually." https://async.com/products/audio-editor, read
  2026-10-17.

  verified. Do not claim either way.**

## Filler words and silences

- Filler: "automatically detects different versions of filler words (think
  'um, uh, mhm') and provides the option to remove each of these words
  individually, or all at once." Preset list: "mmm, um, uh, uhm, uhh, umm,
  mm, mhm." A Words search can "Remove all" instances of any word.
  https://help.async.com/en/articles/6480391-how-do-i-detect-and-remove-filler-words,
  read 2026-10-17.
- Silence Removal: from the audio editor (right-click a clip) or the AI
  Assistant for a whole project, or from the text editor's Silences tab;
  "By default, Async Creator will search for silences longer than 0.5
  seconds, but you can adjust this length".
  https://help.async.com/en/articles/6376539-how-do-i-remove-silences-from-my-audio,
  read 2026-10-17.
- Rough Cut (video): "Rough Cut is an AI-powered editing tool" that removes
  "Filler words", "Retakes" and "Unnecessary silences"; can be run from AI
  Chat in the Video Editor; article dated September 9, 2026.
  https://help.async.com/en/articles/13639873-how-to-use-rough-cut, read
  2026-10-17.
- Marketing page: "Async will handle the rest while smoothing out the
  remaining audio." https://async.com/tools/filler-word-detection, read
  2026-10-17.
