# Descript: the facts posts may state

Every fact below was read on Descript's own pages (descript.com and
help.descript.com) on 17 October 2026, and re-read on 17 October 2026 in a
fact-check pass (raw HTML downloaded and read, including the plan comparison's
"Included" / "Not included" icon alt text). The old help.descript.com/hc/...
article URLs now redirect to new paths; both are given. A post about Descript
states only what is here. Anything marked **unverified** must not be stated.

## Pricing (https://www.descript.com/pricing, read 2026-10-17)

- Prices are shown with a `$` sign. The page does not name the currency
  (presumably US dollars, but the page does not say so; posts should write
  "$" and say prices are shown in dollars).
- Each paid plan shows two figures "per person / month". The page does not
  label which is monthly and which is annual; the "Get started" links for the
  lower figures carry `selected_interval=year`, and the toggle says annual is
  "Saving up to 35% over monthly plan". The HTML shows the Annual button
  pressed by default and the lower figure as the visible one (the higher is
  `aria-hidden`). So: lower figure = billed annually, higher = billed monthly.
  The figures themselves are still not labelled monthly/annual in text.
- The page meta description: "Descript pricing starts free, with paid plans
  from $16/month."

## Media minutes and AI credits
(https://help.descript.com/hc/en-us/articles/27841674958221-Track-and-understand-your-media-minutes-and-AI-credits,
now redirects to https://help.descript.com/billing-payments-plans/track-and-understand-your-media-minutes-and-ai-credits, read 2026-10-17)

- Media minutes are used by uploading audio or video, recording in Descript
  (including Rooms, screen, webcam, microphone), and still images ("Still
  images count as 1 second each").
- AI credits are used by Underlord, Studio Sound, Remove filler words, Green
  Screen, Eye Contact, AI speech, avatars, generated video and generated
  layout packs. Chatting with Underlord: "each message still uses a few AI
  credits."
- "Unused Media minutes and AI Credits don't roll over into the next cycle."
- "Your allowance resets when your subscription renews at the start of each
  billing cycle."
- Rooms: media minutes are used "only for the total length of the session,
  not per participant" (dash in original replaced).
- Credit costs: features applied manually have a cost that "scales with the
  size of the job" (longer media, more filler words removed = more credits).
  "Studio Sound and Remove Filler Words have a max cost of 30 credits per
  file/tool application." Underlord costs are "non-deterministic"; "There's
  no fixed price per action". The Usage tab shows what each run cost.
- "Top ups are shared across your Drive and are available for one-time use
  without changing your subscription plan."
- The page does not say whether editing or exporting cost anything, and does
  not address re-transcription. **Unverified:** whether re-exporting or
  re-editing ever costs anything. Do not claim either way beyond "the page
  lists uploads, recordings and AI features as what uses allowance".

## Text-based editing (https://www.descript.com/features, read 2026-10-17)

- "Text-based editing lets you edit video and audio by editing the
  transcript." "Deleting a word removes that moment of media; moving a
  sentence moves the footage with it."
- "Edit your words to edit your video. ... The timeline is only there if you
  want it."
- Describes Descript as "an AI video and audio editor".
- Headline features listed: Underlord (AI co-editor driven by text
  instructions), text-based editing, captions, screen recording, Eye Contact,
  Green Screen, Create Clips, translation (dubbing and subtitles), Studio
  Sound, Remove Filler Words, Remove Retakes, Regenerate (and Video
  Regenerate), Rooms, transcription, templates, podcasting, integrations,
  Descript MCP.

## Remove filler words
(https://help.descript.com/hc/en-us/articles/10164806394509-Remove-filler-words,
now redirects to https://help.descript.com/script-editing/filler-words, read 2026-10-17)

- Descript underlines common filler words in light blue. Open the AI Tools
  panel and choose "Remove filler words"; a sidebar lists each detected
  instance with timestamp and audio preview.
- Examples named: "um", "uh", "you know". No full list given.
- Per-word options: Delete; Delete and replace with gap; Ignore ("Strikes
  through the filler word in the script but removes the audio"); Remove from
  transcript (script only, audio unchanged).
- "Avoid harsh cuts" setting "automatically skips any filler words that can't
  be removed without clipping into nearby words or leaving behind an awkward
  cut".
- "This feature currently detects filler words in English transcripts only."
- "On current plans, this feature uses AI Credits."
- The article does not say whether it detects repeated words or false starts
  (the pricing table's tooltip mentions "repeated words"; false starts are not
  mentioned anywhere read).

## Edit for Clarity
(https://help.descript.com/hc/en-us/articles/36841959272717-Edit-for-Clarity,
now redirects to https://help.descript.com/script-editing/edit-for-clarity, read 2026-10-17)

- "analyzes your script to clean up pacing, tighten delivery, and remove
  unnecessary or off-topic content."
- Intensity: Low, Medium or Heavy.
- Cuts are kept as "ignored text" for review; ignored text can be restored,
  and edits can be discarded.
- "Run Edit for Clarity when a script rambles, repeats itself, or wanders off
  topic."
- With "Reword for flow" on, it can "regenerate the audio with AI speech so
  the edits sound seamless". Steps say "Turn on Reword for flow to generate AI
  speech where needed"; the default state of the toggle is not stated.
  Requires speaker labels with an AI Speaker assigned to each. AI-generated
  edits appear in blue.
- "On current plans, this feature uses AI Credits."
- The page does not mention language limits. **Unverified:** languages.

## Regenerate
(https://help.descript.com/hc/en-us/articles/13832955606413-Use-Regenerate-to-change-what-was-said,
now redirects to https://help.descript.com/script-editing/change-meaning, titled
"Use Regenerate to edit or change recorded audio", read 2026-10-17)

- With a custom AI speaker, it can "generate text-to-speech audio to fix a
  flubbed delivery, a misspeak, or a wrong spoken word".
- Highlight script, Regenerate (or press D), type new words; new audio is
  generated in the assigned AI speaker.
- The original speaker "must have a custom AI speaker created and authorized
  for use in Descript". Stock voices not supported.
- "Regenerate only supports English audio." Up to 250 characters per action.
- The custom-speaker requirement is stated for changing what was said. The
  same article points to a separate workflow, "Use Regenerate to fix or
  smooth out any jumpy, abrupt, or awkward audio", not read. The features
  page: "Regenerate repairs damaged or mismatched audio by generating
  replacement speech that matches the surrounding tone." Do not say every use
  of Regenerate needs a custom voice.

## Custom voice clones
(https://help.descript.com/ai-speech/custom-speaker, read 2026-10-17)

- "Descript requires explicit recorded authorization from the person whose
  voice will be used before creating a voice clone."
- Will not clone a deceased individual, a non-consenting person, someone
  unable to record the consent statement, or AI or artificial audio.
- "Voice clones are generated using a model based on US English
  pronunciation, so accents may not always be preserved accurately."
- "The consent statement must be read in English, even if you're creating
  non-English text-to-speech audio."
- Uses: typing text to generate audio in your voice, fixing a line without
  re-recording, building a voice-over from scratch.

## Studio Sound
(https://help.descript.com/effects-animations-transitions/studio-sound, read 2026-10-17)

- "enhances spoken voice by reducing background noise, echo, and other
  distractions." "applied at the file level"; to treat part of a file you
  duplicate that section into a new composition. Has an Intensity slider.
- "Turn on Studio Sound when a recording sounds noisy, echoey, or like it was
  captured on a laptop mic."
- Max 30 AI credits per file/tool application (media minutes article).
- "this feature uses AI Credits."

## Rooms (remote recording) (https://www.descript.com/rooms, read 2026-10-17)

- "Descript Rooms is a remote recording studio that runs in your browser."
- "Everyone records locally in up to 4K." "Every participant gets a separate
  file." Uploaded during the session with a cloud backup.
- "For business and enterprise users, Control Room lets up to 10 producers run
  the recording session." (Pricing table: 3 producers on Hobbyist and
  Creator, 10 on Business.)
- "Files record locally and upload as you go, with a cloud backup of every
  participant." "so a shaky connection doesn't ruin the session".

## Transcription languages
(https://help.descript.com/hc/en-us/articles/10249408168845-Supported-transcription-languages,
now redirects to https://help.descript.com/script-editing/supported-transcription-languages, read 2026-10-17)

- Page states 26 languages; the table lists 27 entries: Brazilian Portuguese,
  Catalan, Croatian, Czech, Danish, Dutch, English (UK), English (US),
  Finnish, French (FR), German, Greek (Beta), Hindi (Beta), Hungarian,
  Italian, Latvian, Lithuanian, Malay, Norwegian, Polish, Romanian, Slovak,
  Slovenian, Spanish (US), Swedish, Turkish, Welsh (Beta). English (UK) and
  Welsh must be selected manually.
- "Every supported language is written in the Latin alphabet." The same
  page lists Greek (Beta) and Hindi (Beta), which are not normally written
  in the Latin alphabet. Internal inconsistency: do not write "all Latin
  alphabet" as a flat fact; attribute it or leave it out.
- Descript can "auto-detect the language" or let you set it; files can be
  re-transcribed in a different language.
- English-only: filler word detection, Import transcript, Replace transcript,
  transcription glossary.
- "Descript supports transcription in only one language per file."
- The pricing page says "25-language transcription". Posts should say
  "around 25" or quote a source, not pick a number silently.

## Audio export
(https://help.descript.com/hc/en-us/articles/10255754857741-Export-an-audio-file,
now redirects to https://help.descript.com/export-and-share/audio, read 2026-10-17)

- "Choose from .m4a, .wav, or .mp3."
- Mono or stereo; 44100 or 48000 Hz; bitrate 32 to 256 kbps (M4A and MP3);
  normalise: Off, Peak, -14, -16, -18, -23, -24 LUFS.
- FLAC and Opus are not listed as export options.
- Video export resolutions are on the pricing page (720p Free, 1080p
  Hobbyist, 4K Creator and Business).
