# CapCut pricing: what Pro costs and what it unlocks

**Bottom line:** CapCut is free to start, and when we checked its pricing page from the UK on
17 October 2026 it showed **Pro at £21.99 a month or £199.99 a year** and **Ultra at £58 a month
or £669 a year**. CapCut says prices vary by region, device and promotion, so treat those as what
we saw rather than what you will pay. If what you want is mainly to tidy up your own talking-head
recording by its transcript, Paraphrase Premium is £13.99 a month, but it does far less than
CapCut, so price is only half the question.

CapCut is a full video editor: templates, effects, transitions, captions, AI tools and more, on
desktop, mobile and the web. This post is for people who use it, or are considering it, mainly to
edit talking-head and short-form video by its transcript, and want to know what that costs.

## What CapCut costs

CapCut's [pricing page](https://www.capcut.com/pricing) showed these figures in pounds when we
read it on 17 October 2026:

| Plan | Monthly | Yearly | What the page lists |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Free | £0 | £0 | Free templates, basic editing tools |
| Pro | £21.99 | £199.99 | 1,200 monthly AI credits, AI features, all premium templates, all premium Pro features, 1 TB storage |
| Ultra | £58 | £669 | 6,464 monthly AI credits, everything in Pro plus exclusive AI features, 1 TB storage |
| Teams | Per seat | Per seat | Pro features for every seat, brand kit, 1 TB storage |

Three honest caveats:

- **CapCut says these are not fixed.** The page itself says "Displayed pricing is for reference
  only. Sign in to see final pricing, taxes, and any applicable offers for your account." Its help
  centre says the price of Pro "can vary depending on your region, device, and available
  promotions", and tells you to check from the Upgrade button on the web, the subscription button
  on desktop, or "Join CapCut Pro" in the mobile app.
- **We are not quoting Teams prices.** The per-seat figures on the page did not add up
  consistently across seat counts when we read it, so we would rather send you to the page than
  repeat a number that may be a display error.
- **Other CapCut pages disagree.** A CapCut resource article dated September 2026 quotes Pro in
  dollars at a different price, and a help article gives Pro's cloud storage as 100GB while the
  pricing page says 1 TB. We have used the pricing page. Check your own account before you buy.

## What Pro unlocks

From CapCut's own pages, Pro is mostly about premium assets, AI features and export:

- **Premium templates, effects and assets.** CapCut's help centre says "Both plans unlock all
  premium features, including text templates, video effects, body effects, filters".
- **AI credits.** Pro lists 1,200 monthly AI credits and the option to buy more, which covers
  things like CapCut's AI video generation.
- **Export without the Pro watermark.** This is the part worth understanding. CapCut's help centre
  says you are prompted to join Pro when "your project contains one or more premium elements or
  settings", and that "watermark removal on videos using Pro materials always requires Pro,
  regardless of resolution". On desktop, it says the 4K, 60fps and No Watermark export options
  appear locked for non-Pro users. Swapping premium stickers, transitions or filters for free ones
  can remove the restriction.

## Is transcript editing and filler word removal free?

This is the question most readers of this post actually have, and CapCut's own pages do not give
one clear answer.

- CapCut's [transcript editing page](https://www.capcut.com/tools/video-transcript-editing)
  describes it as "free and online".
- Its [filler words page](https://www.capcut.com/tools/filler-words) says "You can remove filler
  words from the video or audio for free with CapCut's trial", which suggests it may sit behind
  Pro once a trial ends.
- A CapCut article on Standard versus Pro says auto captions are "exclusive to Pro users", and the
  documented desktop filler word workflow starts in Auto captions.

We could not find a pricing page or help centre article that settles it, so we are not going to
tell you either way. Open a project, try it, and see whether the export asks you to join Pro.

## Trial, renewal and refunds

From CapCut's help centre, read 17 October 2026:

- **A 7 day free trial of Pro** for eligible new users. Not offered in all countries, and not if
  you have ever had Pro before, including a past trial. On the web it needs a payment method.
- **Cancel at least 24 hours before renewal**, or "your account will be charged".
- **Refunds are generally not offered.** CapCut says subscriptions "are considered virtual goods,
  and generally, purchases do not support refunds", with appeals for cases such as being billed
  twice.

## How Paraphrase prices transcript editing

Paraphrase is narrower than CapCut and priced differently. It is a browser app that edits your own
voice recording, or a talking-head video, by its word-timed transcript. One plan, priced in
pounds, checked against our live billing on 17 October 2026:

| Billing cycle | Price | Works out at |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Monthly | £13.99 | £13.99 a month |
| Annual | £83.85 | about £6.99 a month |
| Three years | £179.65 | about £4.99 a month |

What that buys:

- **120 hours of audio a month** to transcribe, resetting each billing period.
- **30 AI cleanups a day.** One button proposes the cut: filled pauses, false starts, hedges and
  repetition. It is a proposal, so every cut shows struck through and any word can be put back.
- **Projects up to 4 hours.**
- **Credits are spent once, at transcription.** One credit is one second of audio. Editing,
  cleanup and export do not use credits, so re-editing and re-exporting cost nothing.

The **free plan** gives every new account 15 minutes of transcription with no card, 3 cleanups a
day and projects up to 5 minutes, which is enough for a few short-form clips. There is a 7 day
Premium trial on the monthly and annual cycles (card required), and credit packs from £3.99 for 4
hours that do not expire while your account is open. Details are on the [pricing page](/pricing)
and in [credits and plans](/docs/credits-and-plans).

There is no watermark tier and no premium asset library, because there are no assets: Paraphrase
exports your recording with pieces cut out. With a video take, it exports MP4 with picture and
sound cut at the same words, and it also exports WAV, FLAC, MP3, M4A and Opus. See
[exporting](/docs/exporting).

## Comparing the two honestly

The headline prices are not comparing like with like.

- **CapCut's free plan may be all you need.** If its transcript editing does what you want on your
  footage without any premium elements, you may not need to pay anyone, including us.
- **CapCut Pro buys a creative toolkit.** Templates, effects, AI credits, AI video generation and
  unrestricted export. Paraphrase has none of that: no captions, no effects, no music, no
  templates.
- **Paraphrase buys one job done carefully.** Editing what you said, with a cleanup you review, in
  a tool that never generates speech, so nothing in your export is a word you did not say.

Plenty of people will want both: cut the talking in Paraphrase, export the MP4, then caption and
style it in CapCut. Our [CapCut review](/blog/capcut-review) covers what it is like on documented
behaviour, the [Paraphrase vs CapCut comparison](/blog/paraphrase-vs-capcut) goes through the
editing side, and [CapCut alternatives](/blog/capcut-alternatives) covers the wider field.

The easiest way to compare is on one of your own clips. [Create a free Paraphrase
account](https://paraphrase.bot), no card needed, and see what the cleanup proposes on 15 minutes
of your own talking.
