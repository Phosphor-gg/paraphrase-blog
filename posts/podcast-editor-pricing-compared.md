# Podcast editor pricing compared: what each tool really costs

**Bottom line:** **Audacity** costs nothing: it is free and open source with no paid edition.
For automatic clean-up, **Cleanvoice** starts at **$11 (€10) a month for 10 hours** and unused
subscription hours roll over, up to three times the monthly amount. For a recording studio with
editing, **Podcastle (now Async)** starts at **£11.99 a month on yearly billing** and **Riverside**
at **£24 a month billed yearly (£288 a year)**, as shown to a UK visitor. **Descript** starts at
**$16 a person a month billed annually**. **Adobe Podcast** has a free plan, and its paid features
now come with **Adobe Firefly Pro**, shown at **$19.99 a month** on Adobe Podcast's plans page.
**Paraphrase** (ours) is **£13.99 a month for 120 hours of transcription**. Among the entry plans
priced in pounds that state an hour allowance, that is the lowest cost per hour, but it only edits
your recording by transcript, so it is cheap per hour because it does less. All prices were read
on 17 October 2026, in the currency each page showed, and are not converted, so we do not compare
prices across currencies.

Headline prices hide the number that decides your bill: what each plan meters. Some count hours of
media, some hours of processing, some AI credits, some hours per day, some nothing at all. This post puts the
published prices side by side and then does the arithmetic.

Cleanvoice's own page shows the same per-hour figures, $1.10 (€1) monthly and $0.92 (€0.83)
yearly. Riverside's row divides by download hours, not recording or editing hours, which Riverside
says are unlimited, so treat it as the price of separate tracks rather than of editing.

The "hours" are not the same thing, which matters more than the decimals:

- **Paraphrase** counts seconds transcribed. Editing, AI cleanup and export use no credits, so
  re-editing is free. AI cleanup has its own daily limit instead: 3 runs a day on the free plan and
  30 on Premium.
- **Descript** media hours count "uploaded media files and media recorded in Descript, regardless
  of whether they're transcribed", and AI features such as Studio Sound and Remove filler words
  draw on separate AI credits, up to 30 credits per file each time you apply them.
- **Podcastle** has separate hour limits for transcription, recording and text to speech, plus
  credits for AI generation.
- **Cleanvoice** bills on file duration processed, rounded up to the minute: a 10 minute 20 second
  file costs 11 minutes. Each processing run is billed separately, so reprocessing a file costs
  again.
- **Riverside** has no editing hours to divide by. Its help centre says recording, editing and
  exports are unlimited "(just not the separate tracks!)"; its paid plans' hour limit is on
  downloading separate per-speaker tracks, which is what the row above divides by.
- **CapCut** has no hour figure at all; it meters AI credits.
- **Adobe Podcast** meters per day, not per month: Firefly Pro allows Enhance Speech on up to 4
  hours a day, with files up to 2 hours long, and Studio projects up to 2 hours with no download
  limit. A daily cap is a ceiling, not an allowance you pay for, so we have not turned it into a
  cost per hour. The plan's 4,000 generative credits a month are for Firefly's generative tools.

For scale: a weekly one-hour show is four or five hours of finished audio a month, and raw
recordings may run longer. Every entry plan with an hour allowance covers four or five hours, so
for a show like that the monthly fee, not the per-hour rate, is what you actually pay.

## Each tool's pricing in brief

### Audacity

Free, open source under GPL v3, and its site says it "will always remain free for everyone". Money
only comes in for optional extras: paid cloud storage on audio.com, and paid plugins among the free
and paid options on MuseHub. We could not read the prices of either. Full breakdown: [Audacity pricing](/blog/audacity-pricing).

### Cleanvoice

Pay-as-you-go packs are $11 (€10) for 5 hours, $20 (€18) for 10 and $45 (€40) for 30, valid for
two years. Subscriptions are $11 (€10), $28 (€26) or $90 (€85) a month for 10, 30 or 100 hours.
Unused subscription hours roll over up to three times the monthly amount, every plan includes every
feature, and files are deleted after seven days. Each processing run is billed, so reprocessing a
file costs again. Prices are listed without VAT. One inconsistency:
Cleanvoice's llms.txt file mentions a 200-hour pack at €180 that does not appear on the pricing
page, so we do not count it. See [Cleanvoice pricing](/blog/cleanvoice-pricing).

### Podcastle (now Async)

Essentials £19.99, Pro £39.99 and Teams £84.99 per creator a month, or £11.99, £23.99 and £49.99 a
month on yearly billing; Business is custom. The free plan's limits are lifetime, not monthly.
Inconsistencies: the free column showed "$0.00" while every paid price showed pounds; the Essentials
card says 4 hours of recording while the comparison table says 2; and the trial length is not
stated. There is a 7-day refund policy on new subscriptions. See
[Podcastle pricing](/blog/podcastle-pricing).

### Paraphrase

Ours, so weigh accordingly. Premium is £13.99 a month, £83.85 a year (about £6.99 a month) or
£179.65 for three years (about £4.99 a month), with 120 hours of transcription a month that does
not roll over, 30 AI cleanups a day and projects up to 4 hours. Credit packs that never expire
while your account is open cost £3.99 for 4 hours, £12.99 for 13 hours 20 minutes and £34.99 for
40 hours. A new account on the free plan gets 15 minutes of transcription, three cleanups a day,
and projects up to 5 minutes. A 7-day Premium trial (monthly or annual billing) needs a card. If a
recording fails for a reason on our side, its credits are returned automatically; a file that
cannot be read at all is not refunded. Details on our [pricing page](/pricing) and in
[credits and plans](/docs/credits-and-plans).

### Descript

Hobbyist, Creator and Business are $16, $24 and $50 a person a month billed annually, or $24, $35
and $65 billed monthly, with 10, 30 and 40 media hours. Unused hours and AI credits "don't roll
over". The page shows "$" without naming the currency and does not label which figure is monthly;
we read the lower one as annual from its billing links and its "Saving up to 35%" toggle. See
[Descript pricing](/blog/descript-pricing).

### CapCut

From the UK the page showed Pro at £21.99 a month or £199.99 a year, and Ultra at £58 or £669.
CapCut says pricing "can vary depending on your region, device, and available promotions", and its
pages disagree: Teams per-seat figures were inconsistent, storage is 1 TB on the pricing page but
100GB in a help article, and a CapCut blog post quotes $19.99 a month. Subscriptions are generally
non-refundable. See [CapCut pricing](/blog/capcut-pricing).

### Riverside

As shown to a UK visitor: Pro £35, Grow £45 and Webinar £99 a month, or £24, £34 and £79 a month
billed yearly (£288, £408 and £948 a year); Business is "Contact Sales". Separate-track downloads
are 15, 20 and 25 hours a month. A 14-day trial needs a card. Riverside says it adjusts pricing by
region and accepts USD, EUR and GBP; the page rendered pounds for us, and its static FAQ
quotes different dollar figures, so we do not quote dollar or euro prices. See
[Riverside pricing](/blog/riverside-pricing).

### Adobe Podcast

Adobe stopped selling standalone Adobe Podcast Premium to new customers on 30 August 2026. Its
plans page now offers "the Adobe Firefly Pro plan with access to Adobe Podcast Premium" at $19.99 a
month, without saying whether tax is included or showing an annual price. Adobe's UK Firefly plans
page shows Firefly Pro at £19.99 a month including VAT, with 4,000 generative credits a month that
do not roll over. Adobe offers a free trial; its pages disagree on the length. The free plan keeps
Enhance Speech (up to an hour a day, files up to 30 minutes) and Studio (two project downloads a
day, up to 30 minutes each). Firefly Pro also includes Firefly's Generate Speech, which Adobe
Podcast itself does not offer. See [Adobe Podcast pricing](/blog/adobe-podcast-pricing).

## Why trust these figures

Every competitor price was read on the vendor's own pricing page and help centre on 17 October
2026, not taken from a comparison site. Async's, Riverside's and Adobe Podcast's pages were
rendered in a browser so that their prices loaded; CapCut, Async and Riverside displayed pounds to
us from the UK, and all three may localise. Where a vendor's
pages contradicted each other we said so above instead of picking a number.

Paraphrase is ours. Among the plans priced in pounds, it has the lowest cost per hour of
transcription because it does one job: no recording studio, no noise removal, no video suite, no
generated speech. Where it leads is that
editing and re-editing never cost anything once a recording is transcribed. We have no customer
reviews to cite.

## So what should you pay?

- **Nothing:** Audacity, if you will cut by hand on a waveform.
- **Automatic clean-up of finished episodes:** Cleanvoice, priced by hours processed.
- **Recording remote guests:** Podcastle (now Async), Riverside or Adobe Podcast, whichever plan's
  limits fit.
- **Rescuing noisy speech:** Adobe Podcast's free Enhance Speech, or Firefly Pro for more a day.
- **One suite for audio and video:** Descript.
- **Editing your own voice by transcript, often and repeatedly:** Paraphrase.
