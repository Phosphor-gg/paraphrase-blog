# The best podcast editing software in 2026

**Bottom line:** if your show has remote guests, **Riverside** covers the most of the job on its
own pages, from recording separate local tracks to editing by transcript to hosting and publishing.
**Descript** has the broadest editor if you also make video, with its own remote recording studio.
**Adobe Podcast** records remote guests, edits by transcript and has Enhance Speech for noise and
echo. **Audacity** is free and open source if you are happy editing a waveform. For a solo or
narrated show where you want to cut your own words by transcript and be certain nothing in the edit
was generated, **Paraphrase** (ours) is built for exactly that and nothing more.

Podcast editing is really four jobs: getting a clean recording, cutting it, cleaning the sound, and
getting it out to listeners. Most tools are strong at one or two. This list ranks them on **how
much of that chain each one handles**, going by what each documents, so a narrow tool ranks lower
even when it is strong at its one part. We say where that happens.

## 1. Riverside: record, edit and publish in one place

Riverside's strength is the recording. Its help centre says high-quality tracks "are recorded
locally on each participant's computer or device" so "slow internet should not affect their
quality", with a separate track per person. The editor then lets you cut by transcript, remove
filler words and pauses, remove background noise, normalise audio, add music and captions, and
export your timeline to Premiere Pro or Final Cut Pro. Pro adds podcast hosting and analytics, and
publishing to YouTube, Spotify, Apple and others.

One thing to read carefully: separate-track downloads are capped by plan, at 15 hours a month on
Pro, 20 on Grow and 25 on Webinar. Pro is £35 a month, or £24 a month billed yearly (£288), as
shown to a UK visitor.

**Best for:** interview and panel shows with remote guests. Our [Riverside review](/blog/riverside-review)
has the detail.

## 2. Descript: a broad editor, especially with video

As an editor Descript covers a lot: transcript editing for audio and video, multitrack
transcription with speaker detection, Studio Sound for noise and echo, filler-word and retake
removal, captions, and Rooms for remote recording with a separate file per participant. Edit for
Clarity can tighten a whole episode at the intensity you choose and keeps the cuts as "ignored
text" you can restore.

It also generates speech if you want it: text to speech, Regenerate and custom voice clones, which
need recorded consent from the person whose voice it is. Filler-word detection works on English
transcripts only, and Studio Sound and Remove Filler Words each cost up to 30 AI credits per file
per run.

**Best for:** video podcasts and anyone who wants one editor for everything. Read our
[Descript review](/blog/descript-review).

## 3. Adobe Podcast: recording, transcript editing and Enhance Speech

Adobe Podcast Studio (labelled beta) records remote guests by link, with each participant's audio
"captured directly in their browser and continuously saved" and "separate, high-quality tracks for
each speaker" afterwards. You edit by transcript: "simply delete text in the transcript to remove
the corresponding audio or video from your project." It can detect speakers, and has captions, a
royalty-free music library and audiograms. Its other tool, Enhance Speech, removes noise and echo
and is a genuine strength, with a free plan of up to an hour a day.

Adobe's guides have you find filler words in the transcript and cut the ones you choose yourself.
Studio "is only supported on Google Chrome (desktop)", and on the free plan you can download two
Studio projects a day of up to 30 minutes, without the speaker-separated originals. Standalone
Premium is no longer sold to new customers; it now comes with Adobe Firefly Pro, which also
includes Firefly's Generate Speech.

**Best for:** shows that need their sound rescued as much as their words cut. See our
[Adobe Podcast review](/blog/adobe-podcast-review) and
[Paraphrase vs Adobe Podcast](/blog/paraphrase-vs-adobe-podcast).

## 4. Paraphrase: cutting a solo show by its words

Paraphrase is ours, so weigh this accordingly. It ranks fourth on this list's axis because it
covers one link in the chain: cutting. On that link it leads in three specific ways.

- **Every AI cut is reviewable word by word.** One button proposes cuts for filled pauses, false
  starts, hedges and repetition, including the same point made twice in different words. Each one
  shows struck through in the transcript and any word can be restored. See
  [how AI cleanup works](/docs/ai-cleanup).
- **It never generates speech**, so what listeners hear is what you said.
- **Re-editing is free.** Credits are spent once, at transcription; editing, cleanup and export
  use none. Cleanup has a daily limit: 3 runs on the free plan, 30 on Premium.

What it does not do matters for podcasts: no remote recording, no multitrack or speaker labels, no
noise removal, no hosting. It suits a single recording of one voice, or a recording you are
happy to cut as one track. More on [editing podcasts with Paraphrase](/podcast-editing).

**Best for:** solo, narrated and scripted shows recorded in one take or several.

## 5. Audacity: free and open source

Audacity is free and open source (GPL v3), and its homepage says it "will always remain free for
everyone". It records and edits on the waveform with unlimited undo, and its Cut feature page names
"Remove ums, coughs, and long pauses from spoken recordings" as a podcast use. Noise Reduction works
best on steady noise like hiss and hum, and it has a compressor. You do every cut by hand, which
takes time but leaves every decision to you.

**Best for:** anyone on no budget who is comfortable on a waveform. See our
[Audacity review](/blog/audacity-review).

## How to choose podcast editing software

- **Guests on the call?** Start with the recording: Riverside, Descript's Rooms, or Adobe Podcast
  Studio.
- **Filmed episodes?** Descript.
- **Noisy rooms?** Adobe Podcast's Enhance Speech, Descript's Studio Sound, or Audacity's Noise
  Reduction for steady noise.
- **No budget?** Audacity, and accept the time it takes.
- **Solo show, and you care exactly which words stay?** Paraphrase, possibly alongside one of the
  above for recording or sound.
