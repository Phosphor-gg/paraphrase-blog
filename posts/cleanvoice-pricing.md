# Cleanvoice pricing: plans, credits and what an hour costs

**Bottom line:** Cleanvoice charges by **hours of processed audio**. Subscriptions start at **$11
(€10) a month for 10 hours**, pay-as-you-go packs start at **$11 (€10) for 5 hours** and last two
years, every plan includes every feature, and there are 30 free minutes with no card. Paraphrase
is £13.99 a month for 120 hours of transcription, with editing, cleanup and export not using
credits, but it buys a different thing: word-by-word editing of what you said rather than automatic
sound clean-up.

Prices below were read on Cleanvoice's [pricing page](https://cleanvoice.ai/pricing/) on 17 October
2026. The page has a USD and EUR toggle, so both are given. Prices are listed without VAT, and
Cleanvoice says sales tax or VAT may be added depending on where you live. Vendors change pricing,
so check before you buy.

## The free trial

Cleanvoice gives you **30 minutes of processed audio free, with no credit card**. Its help centre
says the 30 minutes are added when you sign up and do not expire. You can also try a guest upload
without signing up, but guest audio over 30 minutes (or video over 10 minutes) is trimmed and you
only review part of the result until you sign up. The pricing page marks the trial as including all
features. That is enough to put a real segment of an episode through every setting and hear the
result, which is the right way to judge a tool like this.

## Subscription plans

| Hours a month | Monthly (USD) | Monthly (EUR) | Yearly (USD) | Yearly (EUR) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 10 | $11 | €10 | $110 | €100 |
| 30 | $28 | €26 | $300 | €260 |
| 100 | $90 | €85 | $900 | €850 |

That works out at $1.10 an hour on the smallest monthly plan, falling to $0.90 an hour at 100
hours. Paying yearly costs exactly ten months' worth on every plan in euros; in dollars the same
is true of the 10 and 100 hour plans, and the 30 hour plan costs slightly more ($300 against
$280).

**Unused hours roll over**, up to three times your monthly allowance. Cleanvoice's own example: on
the 10 hour plan you get 10 hours each month and can carry up to 30 hours from previous months. If
you cancel, unused subscription credits last until the end of the billing period. You can upgrade,
downgrade or cancel at any time.

For 200 or more hours a month there is a custom plan "starting at $0.01 / minute" with custom API
endpoints and priority support, arranged on a call.

## Pay-as-you-go packs

| Hours | USD | EUR | Per hour (USD) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 5 | $11 | €10 | $2.20 |
| 10 | $20 | €18 | $2.00 |
| 30 | $45 | €40 | $1.50 |

Pack credits are **valid for two years**, which makes them sensible for occasional use. If you have
both, subscription hours are spent first and pack hours after.

## How usage is counted

A few details from Cleanvoice's pricing FAQ that change the real cost:

- **Billing is by the length of each file, rounded up to the minute, with a one minute minimum.**
  Their example: a 10 minute 20 second file is billed as 11 minutes. For long episodes this is
  noise. For lots of short clips it adds up.
- **Multitrack uploads currently cost nothing extra**, though Cleanvoice says this will change.
- **Failed uploads are not charged.**
- **Reprocessing is charged again.** Cleanvoice's help centre says each processing run is billed
  separately, so running the same file again with different settings counts as a new edit
  (support may give you a credit if you were unhappy with a result).
- **Every plan includes every feature**: filler words, background noise, silences, video,
  mouth sounds and breaths, the Studio Sound enhancer, timeline export, and transcription with
  summaries. There is no feature tier to climb.

One more thing worth knowing before you plan around it: Cleanvoice stores your original and edited
files for **7 days**, then deletes them permanently. Keep your own copies.

## How Paraphrase prices editing

Paraphrase is a different kind of tool, so its pricing measures something different. Prices
below are from Paraphrase's live billing setup, checked 17 October 2026. It edits your
recording by its transcript: you cut and reorder words, and one AI button proposes a cut you review
word by word. It has no noise removal or other sound processing.

**Credits buy transcription only.** One credit transcribes one second of audio. Editing, AI
cleanup and exporting do not use credits, and because the original recording stays in your account
until you delete it, re-editing and re-exporting cost nothing.

| Plan | Price | Includes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Free | £0, no card | 15 minutes of transcription once, 3 AI cleanups a day, projects up to 5 minutes |
| Premium, monthly | £13.99 a month | 120 hours of audio a month, 30 AI cleanups a day, projects up to 4 hours |
| Premium, annual | £83.85 a year (about £6.99 a month) | The same |
| Premium, three years | £179.65 (about £4.99 a month) | The same |

Premium's 120 hours reset each billing period and **do not roll over**, which is the opposite of
Cleanvoice's subscription. There is a 7 day Premium trial on the monthly and annual cycles, with a
card required, one per account.

**Credit packs** work on either plan and never expire while the account is open: £3.99 for 4
hours, £12.99 for 13 hours 20 minutes, £34.99 for 40 hours. Bear in mind the free plan's 5 minute
project limit, so for full-length episodes you will want Premium. The [credits and plans
docs](/docs/credits-and-plans) explain the details, and the [pricing page](/pricing) has the
current figures.

## Comparing the two honestly

The currencies differ and we have not converted them, so compare the shape rather than the
numbers:

- **For a weekly hour-long show**, Cleanvoice's smallest plan (10 hours a month) covers it with
  room to roll over. Paraphrase Premium covers it many times over, but you are paying for
  headroom you may not use.
- **For heavy use**, Paraphrase's 120 hours a month sit at one price, where Cleanvoice's 100 hour
  plan is its top published subscription. The AI cleanup is capped at 30 runs a day on Premium.
- **Billing granularity**: Cleanvoice rounds each file up to the minute; Paraphrase counts
  seconds.
- **Rollover**: Cleanvoice subscription hours roll over up to three months' worth; Paraphrase
  monthly hours do not, though its packs never expire while the account is open.
- **They are not buying the same thing.** A Cleanvoice hour is an hour of automatic cleaning and
  cutting of sounds. A Paraphrase hour is an hour of transcript you then edit, as often as you
  like, by meaning.

Which is why, for some shows, the honest answer is both: cut the words in Paraphrase, clean the
sound in Cleanvoice. There is a [full comparison](/blog/paraphrase-vs-cleanvoice) of how the two
work, a [Cleanvoice review](/blog/cleanvoice-review), and a roundup of
[Cleanvoice alternatives](/blog/cleanvoice-alternatives).

Try both on your own recording before paying for either. Cleanvoice gives you 30 free minutes, and
a [free Paraphrase account](https://paraphrase.bot) gives you 15 minutes of transcription and three
AI cleanups a day, no card needed.
