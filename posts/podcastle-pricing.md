# Podcastle pricing: what Async costs and what each plan includes

**Bottom line:** Podcastle is now Async, and its paid plans are **Essentials at £19.99 a month**,
**Pro at £39.99** and **Teams at £84.99 per creator**, or £11.99, £23.99 and £49.99 a month on yearly
billing. Each plan sets separate hour limits for transcription, recording and AI voices, plus a
monthly pot of AI credits. The free plan's allowances are lifetime, not monthly. Paraphrase is £13.99 a
month with 120 hours of transcription, but it only edits your recording; it does not record guests,
clean up sound or generate anything.

## Podcastle is Async now

Podcastle.ai/pricing redirects to async.com/pricing, and Async's podcast page is headed "Formerly
Podcastle". So the prices below are Async's. The podcast studio is one part of a wider product with
AI video, image, music and voice generation, which is why the plans are built around **AI credits**
as well as hours.

All prices were read on Async's pricing page on **17 October 2026**. Two caveats:

- **They displayed to us in pounds.** The page may show your local currency and a different figure.
- **Prices change.** Check the page before you buy.

One inconsistency worth flagging: the Essentials plan card says "4 hours of Audio & Video
Recording", while the comparison table's remote multitrack recording row says 2 hours. We cannot
tell from the page which applies to what, so check with Async if recording hours on Essentials
matter to you.

Every paid plan card lists "AI Audio enhancement: Magic Dust, Noise reduction, Silence removal";
the comparison table does not include them on Free.
Essentials and Pro get AI subtitles in English only; Teams gets "5+ languages", plus AI dubbing and
lip sync, Producer Mode and a brand kit. Session participants are capped at 10 on every plan.

Several features in the comparison table are shown as ticks rather than numbers. Plan by plan:

- **Text-based audio editing:** "Limited" on Free, included on every paid plan. The page does not
  say what "Limited" means.
- **Voice cloning:** Pro, Teams and Business only, not Free or Essentials.
- **Podcast hosting and publishing:** every plan, including Free.
- **Watermark removal:** paid plans only.
- **Team workspace and collaboration:** Teams and Business only.
- **Producer Mode:** Teams and Business only; branded waiting room widgets from Pro up.

## How the credits work

Credits are the unit for AI generation: video models, images, avatars, music. Async's FAQ is candid
that some usage is hard to predict: when you ask its AI chat to do a broader task, the cost "can
depend on what the AI actually ends up doing". The rules worth knowing, quoted from the FAQ:

- Monthly credits "reset at the start of each billing cycle". They do not roll over.
- On a yearly plan, "credits are added to your balance monthly, not all at once".
- Top-up packs last until the end of a yearly subscription, or two months on a monthly one. The
  page lists top-up packs but did not show us their prices.
- Credits are "non-refundable once used", but are restored automatically if a technical error stops
  a generation.

Transcription, recording and text to speech each have their **own hour limits** in the table above,
separate from credits. If you only edit podcasts, the hours are the numbers to read.

## Free plan, trials and refunds

- **No card** is needed for the free plan.
- The free plan's main allowances are **lifetime**: 2 hours of audio transcription, 1 hour of
  remote recording, about 15 minutes of text to speech and 10 credits. Exports are limited to 1 file,
  audio at up to 160kbps MP3, and the comparison table shows no Magic Dust, noise reduction, silence
  removal or watermark removal on Free. It is a way to try the product, not to run a show.
- **Refunds:** "a 7-day refund policy on new subscriptions, which applies to the initial upgrade
  (excluding AI credits used)".
- **Cancelling:** any time, from Profile Settings. Upgrades are immediate and prorated; downgrades
  take effect at the end of the billing cycle.
- **Payment:** PayPal and major cards.

## How Paraphrase prices editing

Paraphrase has a free plan and one paid plan, Premium, priced for transcription only:

| Billing cycle | Price | Works out at |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Monthly | £13.99 | £13.99 a month |
| Annual | £83.85 | about £6.99 a month |
| Three years | £179.65 | about £4.99 a month |

Premium includes **120 hours of audio a month**, 30 AI cleanups a day and projects up to 4 hours.
One credit transcribes one second of audio, spent once at transcription. Editing, AI cleanup,
re-editing and exporting never cost credits. Monthly hours reset each billing period and do not roll
over; extra credit packs (£3.99 for 4 hours, £12.99 for 13 hours 20 minutes, £34.99 for 40 hours)
never expire while your account is open.

Every new account gets **15 minutes of transcription free, no card**, with 3 AI cleanups a day and
projects up to 5 minutes. There is a 7 day Premium trial on the monthly and annual cycles, card
required. Prices are in GBP and were checked on 17 October 2026. Full details are on the
[pricing page](/pricing) and in [credits and plans](/docs/credits-and-plans).

## Comparing the two honestly

The numbers invite a direct comparison, and it is only half fair.

- **On transcription hours alone, Paraphrase includes more.** 120 hours a month for £13.99 against
  10 hours for £19.99 on Async Essentials, or 25 hours on Pro at £39.99.
- **On everything else, Async includes far more.** Remote multitrack recording, noise reduction and
  levelling, AI voices (and voice cloning from Pro up), subtitles, hosting with an RSS feed, video
  export up to Full HD on Essentials or 4K from Pro, and AI generation credits. Paraphrase does none of those. If you use them, the price buys real things.
- **Languages differ.** Async's help centre lists five transcription languages: English, Spanish,
  German, French and Italian. Paraphrase transcribes 25 European languages.
- **Paraphrase never generates speech.** That is a design choice rather than a missing tier.

So the honest answer depends on what you use. If you record remote guests and publish from the same
tool, Async's plans are buying a whole studio. If you record yourself and spend most of your time
cutting stumbles, you are paying for a lot you do not use.
